Edith — Privacy Policy

Edith is a personal AI agent built and used by a single individual. It is not distributed publicly and does not collect, sell, or share data with third parties.

Health data

When you connect Health Connect via /health login, Edith reads the health data types you explicitly grant (steps, weight, active calories, and workouts) and sends them to Edith's own private backend, where they are stored solely so Edith can answer questions about your health goal progress. This data is never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising, and is only accessible to the app's owner.

You can revoke access at any time from Android's Health Connect app settings, or by opening Health Connect settings from within Edith.
